# Riverside Community Center — Build and Open

The center opens to the public next spring. This plan carries every workstream from the first permit to opening week, and each section has one lead who reports at the Thursday standing meeting.

An item marked done has been signed off by the program manager. Anything waiting on an outside party says so in its notes.
